Most AI tools are rented services. Your conversations, files, workflows, and memory sit behind someone else’s platform. If that platform changes terms, raises prices, adds restrictions, or disappears, your AI life can disappear with it.

SPAI is being built around a different idea: your assistant should belong to you, run locally wherever possible, remember only what you choose, and stay useful as your hardware and open AI models improve.

The assistant should belong to the user.

SPAI’s long-term direction is simple: make personal AI feel owned, safe, extensible, and permanent.

  • No hidden ad model. SPAI is not planned as an ad platform. The product should make money from the product, not from user attention.
  • No forced cloud memory. Your files, chats, and memories should not have to live behind someone else’s platform.
  • No hidden tool access. Apps, files, and automation should be permission-gated and visible to the user.
  • No one-size-fits-all model. SPAI is being designed to support different local models, backends, and hardware profiles.
  • No subscription trap for the core product. The goal is that once you own SPAI, you keep the version you bought.

What the first version is aiming to show.

The first public demo is focused on proving the core idea: a local AI assistant that can chat, use approved tools, open approved apps, read approved files, and feel like the beginning of a real personal AI platform.

First-look demo goals

Local chat, model routing, basic voice, app opening, file reading, hardware visibility, and a polished launcher-style interface.

Long-term platform goals

Plugin marketplace, stronger local automation, advanced memory, document tools, custom voices, personal workflows, and small-business support.
